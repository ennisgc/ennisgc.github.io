  • 25 Nov Added the Winter Song soprano line tape.
  • 25 Nov Every track now shows a possible key: the key it starts in. It is a best guess; major and its relative minor look the same, so both are shown.

  • 25 Nov The keyboard piano now uses real piano recordings instead of a synthesised sound.

  • 25 Nov New audio players: waveform, drag to select a section and repeat it, speed control (0.5x-1.25x), and the last note heard on line tapes.
  • 25 Nov New 2-octave keyboard (button at the top of each song page) that slides in from the left. Tap, slide, or use your computer keyboard. Sounds: Piano (default), Choir (ah) or MIDI synth.

Technical

  • 26 Nov Removed the videos field and YouTube embeds from song.json, song.js, add-song.py and check.py. The original audio is a normal track (label Original Audio, file original-audio.m4a|mp3, section Full Song); check.py warns when it is missing.
  • 25 Nov Added the optional Report an issue form: set reportForm in songs.json (form URL and entry.NNN ids). Buttons stay hidden while it is empty (see README.md).
  • 25 Nov Keys come from tools/audio.py (compute_keys, needs numpy) and are stored in each track's peaks.json entry; make-peaks.py and check.py treat a missing key as out of date. Piano samples (Salamander Grand, CC-BY) are in samples/piano/.
  • 25 Nov Note detection is line tapes only, with a per-voice pitch range, notes stored per 0.1 s slot as note names, and bar height from a tracked octave (details in README.md). Lyrics allow <strong> and <em> only; everything else is escaped.
  • 25 Nov Wrote README.md for people and AI agents (how the site works, data formats, tools, gotchas). Measured note detection: about 90% of windows on clean line tapes, 15-25% on piano+voice tapes, about 7% on backing tracks.
  • 25 Nov Players and keyboard are plain JavaScript (player.js, keyboard.js) plus wavesurfer.js. Waveforms come from peaks.json in each song folder, so audio only downloads when you press play.
  • 25 Nov Re-encoded four line tapes whose audio stream was damaged and wouldn't play in Firefox. check.py --deep now finds these.
  • 25 Nov All audio is now 128 kbps or lower (about 109 MB smaller, so pages load faster). New audio is compressed automatically, and check.py flags anything over the limit.
  • 25 Nov Added tools/add-song.py and tools/check.py for adding songs and catching mistakes (see tools/README.md).
  • 25 Nov Both lists are alphabetical. For a fixed set order, set "performanceOrder": "custom" in songs.json.
  • 25 Nov Added the Song Status table.
  • 25 Nov Christmas label is now a santa-hat image (the emoji didn't show in every browser).
  • 24 Nov Each song lives in its own folder (songs/<song>/) with its audio, song.json and lyrics.txt. Old links still redirect.
  • 24 Nov Song pages and these lists are built in the browser from songs.json.
  • 24 Nov Lyrics show in a side panel on desktop (full-screen pop-up on phones).
  • 24 Nov Fixed broken HTML, the genre filter, and a file-name case bug.
